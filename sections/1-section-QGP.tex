\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]

%--------------------------------------------------------
\column{0.50\textwidth}

\small

Relativistic heavy-ion collisions create extreme conditions where nuclear
matter undergoes a transition to a deconfined state of quarks and gluons:
the \textbf{Quark--Gluon Plasma (QGP)}.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\begin{block}{Research}
\small

Constrain the properties of the Quark--Gluon Plasma by analyzing final-state hadronic observables and reconstructing the complete evolution of relativistic heavy-ion collisions.

\begin{itemize}
\item Initial-state conditions;
\item Relativistic hydrodynamic;
\item Particlization.
\end{itemize}

\end{block}
